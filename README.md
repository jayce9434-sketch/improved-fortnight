# Chitin Conflict - Attempt 2: Frontline Overhaul

Chitin Conflict is an autonomous insect-colony history simulator designed around the fun of watching borders, flags, coalitions, wars, revolts, unions and entire colony-states change over time.

## Run
Open `index.html` in a modern browser, or upload the flat files to a GitHub Pages repository.

## Attempt 2 highlights
- Continuous multi-hotspot wars with rapid local skirmishes instead of only slow, giant territory chunks.
- Full Offensive, Breakthrough, Border Fighting and Stalemate phases.
- Animated combat flashes, projectile streaks, impact bursts, temporary battlefield-death marks and extra sound for major impacts.
- Waving species flags on active frontlines plus two-flag current-war cards.
- Small local captures during normal combat; named Major Battles can create larger breakthroughs.
- Detailed war records with deaths by side, battle counts, coalition members, named battles, offensives, momentum and fictional insect war-crime incident logs.
- World statistics track total wars, recorded deaths, major battles, revolts, extinct colonies, unifications and war-crime reports.
- Automatic diplomacy: colonies can form alliances without player input.
- Alliance members mobilize together in coalition wars.
- Alliance Unity and loyalty systems. Alliances can become Unions and eventually fully unify into a single colony-state.
- Vassal, Puppet and Satellite relationships with autonomy and loyalty.
- Persistent rightful territory, occupied land and later integration.
- Nest-level revolt pressure and breakaway colonies.
- 21 insect factions/species including numerous ants, termites, wasps/hornets, a bee and a beetle.
- 18 procedural flag layouts and species emblems. Each colony can also choose from 12 generated species-banner premades or use the custom 12 x 8 flag painter.
- Eight map presets: Creekside Borders, Picnic Coast, Backyard Basin, Rotwood Expanse, Curbside Archipelago, Pantry Front, Drainage Delta and Boardwalk Beachhead.
- Water, beaches, sand, mud, rivers, islands, stone, logs and narrow strategic crossings/chokepoints.
- Political, Wars, Alliances, Rightful Land, Terrain, Economy and Revolt Risk map modes.
- God Mode tools for territory painting, terrain painting, colony spawning, forced diplomacy, revolts, Major Battles and Frontline Barrages.
- Local saves and JSON world import/export.
- 0-2000% volume control and speeds through 16x.
- Touch-friendly, single-screen map layout for tablets.
- No emojis in the game UI.

The war-crime system is fictional world-history flavor for insect colonies; it is not a recreation of real conflicts.

## GitHub Pages
Keep the files at the repository root, enable GitHub Pages for the repository, and use `index.html` as the entry page. The included manifest and icons support installation-like browser presentation where available.
